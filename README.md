I am an **Electronic Engineer** currently completing my Master's Degree in **Bioengineering (Neurotechnologies track)** at **UNIMORE** (*Università degli Studi di Modena e Reggio Emilia*).

My academic journey is driven by a clear goal: **bridging the gap between hardware engineering and human physiology**.

* 🧠 **Specialization:** Master's Degree in Bioengineering with a focus on neuro-robotics and the central nervous system.
